## Summary

A raw, personal trading psychology reflection about what trading actually does to your brain chemistry. Trading isn't about being smart — it's about chemistry. Dopamine gives the high, cortisol keeps you trapped in stress loops. Humans weren't designed to live under constant pressure, but that's exactly what traders do every day. Cortisol ruins sleep, makes decisions emotional, destroys patience — and you don't even realize you're changing.

---

## Core Arguments

### 1. Trading isn't about being smart — it's about chemistry

- Dopamine gives the high
- Cortisol keeps you trapped in stress loops
- Humans weren't designed to live under constant pressure — but traders do exactly that every day
- Cortisol ruins sleep, makes decisions emotional, destroys patience — and you don't even realize you're changing

### 2. The dangerous loop

```
Small wins → miss runner → fear of missing out → impulsive → lose more → 
lower standards to feel involved → trading becomes addiction
```

### 3. Warning signs:

- Losing 30% doesn't shock you anymore
- Stress becomes part of your personality
- You're not trading to win, you're trading to feel something
- PnL determines whether you deserve peace that day

### 4. The haunting conclusion

> "You've never actually been chasing money. You've been chasing relief."

---

## Key Quote

> "Sometimes the strongest thing a trader can do is absolutely nothing. Not revenge trade. Not deposit more money trying to save the month. Not stare at charts for 16 hours hoping something changes. Just stop."
