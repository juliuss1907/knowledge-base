## Summary

A mental model for better judgment in uncertain situations: Probabilistic Thinking. Instead of viewing outcomes as binary (yes/no), consider them as probabilities. This approach helps you make better decisions by accounting for uncertainty, updating beliefs based on new evidence (Bayesian updating), and avoiding black-and-white thinking.
