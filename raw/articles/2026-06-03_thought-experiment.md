## Summary

A mental tool used by Einstein, Galileo, and modern thinkers: Thought Experiments. By imagining hypothetical scenarios and reasoning through them, you can explore possibilities, test ideas, and gain insights without real-world constraints. Useful for decision-making, problem-solving, and understanding complex systems.
