# Trading Addiction Cycle

## Definition

Vòng lặp nghiện trading — chuỗi phản ứng từ small wins qua FOMO đến impulsive losses, dẫn đến việc lower standards chỉ để feel involved, biến trading thành addiction.

## Key ideas

**The loop:**
```
Small wins → miss runner → fear of missing out → impulsive → lose more → 
lower standards to feel involved → trading becomes addiction
```

**Warning signs you're in the cycle:**
- Losing 30% doesn't shock you anymore
- Stress becomes part of your personality
- Trading to feel something (not to win)
- PnL determines whether you deserve peace

**The realization:**
> "You've never actually been chasing money. You've been chasing relief."

**Exit:** Sometimes the strongest thing is absolutely nothing — không revenge trade, không deposit thêm, không stare charts 16 hours — just stop.
