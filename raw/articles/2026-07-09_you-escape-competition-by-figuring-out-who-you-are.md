# you escape competition by figuring out who you are

**Author:** Erifili
**Source:** Substack
**Published:** 2026-02-09

---

## Core Thesis

Literally nothing works as well as authenticity. You have no one to compete with because no one is you. Your unique amalgamation of perspectives, experiences, influences, taste, and ideas is one in eight billion. There is no real competition when your main goal is to find and bring to life your most authentic creative expression.

---

## The Exhaustion of Imitation

Many creatives are exhausted by having to build a brand on social media — turning work into content, doing things they're not interested in because that's what the world asks for.

> "I've been studying what works for other people and trying to replicate it, but it hasn't really been working."

Trying to deconstruct others' success and give it your own twist never brings real results. What it does accomplish is making you completely drained — a constant feeling of "I'm doing all these right things — it should be working better!"

---

## The Miracle of Authenticity

> "But whenever I came back into myself instead of looking outside, and just created entirely what I felt like creating, little miracles would happen."

**Examples from Erifili's experience:**
- An essay she hesitated to post because its thesis seemed too "obvious" → 800,000 reads
- A video recorded in her garden with bad audio and background noise → went viral, led to new friendships
- Creators she'd admired for years started following her and became friends

> "The little miracles would only happen when I created things with zero pressure, and when I was very far removed from the mindset of trying to compete with anyone."

---

## Social Media Removes You From Yourself

Scrolling social media is nothing but a massive cause of stress: it removes you from yourself and sends you spiraling into loops of everything you *should* or *could* be doing. Even when it feels like "inspiration," it's comparison and anxiety masked as productivity.

> "Every time I'd find myself subconsciously looking at someone else's success and wondering how my work could live up to those standards, I'd stop and instead come back fully into just what I feel like creating."

---

## The Antidote: Self-Knowledge

Social media removes us from ourselves constantly. Without practices like journaling, mindfulness, or restful alone time, your days are spent looking for answers in other people's success instead of developing your own uniqueness.

Recommended: *The Artist's Way* by Julia Cameron — a guided practice that aims to bring you closer to more parts of yourself.

---

## Bottom Line

> "Their success will never take anything away from yours, because the success that's most sustainable will be the one you get to through your most authentic self-expression. The answers to what you 'should' be doing really are inside you; create the thing you most genuinely want to create, with no audience to perform for, and I swear the right audience will come. The more you understand yourself, the more you will be understood."
