# American Security Guarantee

## Definition

Unwritten military protection commitment từ US tới Gulf producers — nền tảng thực sự của petrodollar system, không phải currency arrangement.

## Key ideas

- 1974-1975: Kissinger + King Faisal — web of mutual interests
- US military protection cho Saudi oil fields
- Exchange: protection ↔ dollar pricing + Treasury purchases
- Dollar pricing was RENT cho security guarantee
- Fragmenting: bilateral arrangements replacing multilateral umbrella
