# Reputation Management

## Definition

Reputation management là việc deliberately build và maintain character của mình qua thời gian — vì reputation là một trong few things còn lại sau khi bạn mất. People remembered cho who they were (character), không phải what they accomplished. Reputation đến từ consistency trong behavior, không phải achievements.

## Key ideas

- **Reputation lives after death:** Attend enough funerals và bạn sẽ thấy — person always remembered for character, không phải achievements
- **Never talk behind someone's back:** Gossiping makes you seem less trustworthy to listeners — they'll think you doing same behind their backs
- **Be nice to people on the come up:** You never know where they'll be in future; underestimate someone's potential = missed connection when they succeed
- **Present yourself intentionally:** How you dress, speak, post on social media, carry yourself — think of it like building your own video game character
- **Prioritize reputation:** One of few things that lives on after you die; character > achievements
- **Stand up to bullies:** Don't let anyone push you around — most bullies back down once you stand up for yourself
- **Never disrespect elders:** Their experience can help avoid many mistakes; listen with open mind, then make own decisions
- **Choose partner wisely:** Deep connection > puppy love; solid partnership makes huge difference — take time to choose someone to build future with
- **Be motivated by values, not money:** Strong values = no one can buy you; prioritize family, refuse to compromise principles
- **Use unfair advantages:** Identify strengths/circumstances that set you apart — not cheating, just recognizing what you're naturally good at
