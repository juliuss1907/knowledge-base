# I'm 57. If you're in your 20's please watch this.

## Summary

Video từ người 57 tuổi — doanh nhân tự lập, đã xây dựng đế chế từ việc bắt đầu từ scratch — chia sẻ 50 lời khuyên cho người ở tuổi 20. Tác giả nhấn mạnh rằng success đến từ failures nhiều hơn achievements, và reputation là thứ duy nhất còn lại sau khi bạn mất. Phần lớn lời khuyên tập trung vào financial discipline, personal branding, và cách navigate relationships.

Điểm nổi bật: work on willpower (focus là superpower trong thời đại distractions), be producer không chỉ consumer, stand up to bullies, skip flashy cars (build wealth thay vì status), pay credit card mỗi tháng, invest retirement ngay từ đầu, và choose partner wisely. Nhiều tips cụ thể như 50-20-30-25-5 budget rule, tackle tough tasks in morning, và look after your back health.

## Key points

- Willpower và focus: ability to work hard trong thời đại distractions là superpower — "if you can focus and work hard, you'll be miles ahead"
- Producer > consumer: put more into world than take — otherwise robbed of time and attention
- Network > knowledge: "who you know matters a thousand times more than what you know" — great network beats money in bank
- Do opposite of most people: reject being "normal and vanilla" — if you do what everyone does, you get same results
- Don't be best, be only: learn unrelated skills và combine them (Steve Jobs: calligraphy + technology = Apple)
- Quick reversible decisions, slow irreversible ones: Jeff Bezos framework — biggest risk with reversible decisions is dragging feet
- Financial discipline: 50% needs, 20% savings, 30% debt repayment, then 25% investing + 5% risky plays
- Reputation lives on after death: character > achievements — people remembered for who they were, not what they accomplished
- Skip flashy cars: buy used but reliable — consistently building wealth > short-term splurging
- Choose partner wisely: "I wouldn't be a millionaire without my supportive wife" — deep connection > dating around
- Look after your back: slouching causes issues by mid-30s — no quick fix for back pain unlike dental
