# Trading Psychology

## Definition

Tâm lý học trading — study của cognitive và emotional factors ảnh hưởng đến trading decisions. Không phải về being smart, mà là về managing brain chemistry (dopamine, cortisol) và recognizing psychological traps.

## Key ideas

- **Not about intelligence:** Trading isn't about being smart — it's about chemistry
- **Brain chemistry:** Dopamine (high), cortisol (stress) — hormones drive behavior
- **The dangerous loop:** Wins → FOMO → impulsive → losses → addiction
- **Warning signs:**
  - Losing 30% không shock
  - Stress becomes personality
  - Trading to feel something
  - PnL = peace
- **The solution:** Know when not to trade — sometimes nothing is the strongest move
