24-36 months. That's how long experts think it will take for AI to replace most jobs. White collar — accountants, economists, mathematicians, software engineers.

> AI just created itself.

Coding, as a skill, is no longer valuable. **Writing is the new coding language.**

## The Old System Is Dead

The current education system was developed during the Industrial Revolution to turn humans into tools for factories — obedient workers who clock in, check boxes, ask no questions.

> If you don't create your own work, you will be assigned one. If you don't think for yourself, you will be told what to think about. If you don't build your own life, you will build someone else's.

The old world of building a good life by purely relying on your employer is gone.

## The Idea Economy

Hard work is overrated. You now live in an age of infinite leverage — social media, no-code tools, AI. The content is free. The tools are free. AI is already built. The entire infrastructure to start a one-person business exists.

> The person with the best idea wins.

Ideas are like a virus — you only need a handful of people speaking about your idea before it becomes contagious.

AI has reduced the gap from beginner to execution from months/years to hours/days. Creative individuals can now focus on their zone of genius (creating, ideating, imagining, thinking) and outsource technical parts to AI.

## The 5 Skills AI Can't Replace

Most high-income skills will be replaced by AI in 24 months — research, programming, math, design. You can't outlearn AI.

The solution: lean into what makes humans special — your ability to create.

### Skill 1: Creativity

NASA study (1960s): 98% of kids aged 3-5 were considered geniuses. By adulthood, only 2%.

Schools kill creativity because being creative means not conforming to the system. Creativity is a muscle — it grows the more you use it, atrophies the less you use it.

No one can teach creativity with a step-by-step solution — that would mean adopting their thinking, which isn't creativity. Creativity, by definition, exists outside whatever lines you draw.

Creativity appears differently: poetry, coding, writing, building, design, art, marketing, cooking, baking, research, law.

> "You feel bored, anxious, or overwhelmed because your mind wants ideas to flow but you won't let them." — Dan Koe

The more you create, the more creative you become. The more creative you are, the more future-proof you become — you see the bigger picture and how everything connects in ways even AI can't see.

### Skill 2: Agency

Agency is not "entrepreneur vs employee." It's your ability to set your own goals and keep trying and retrying, regardless of how many times you fail, until you achieve your goal.

To be high-agency you need to be slightly delusional in other people's eyes. For months or years, failing while everyone tells you to quit.

Three components:
- **Learn to hunt** — identify problems
- **Learn to solve** — use AI for technical side, spend energy on big-picture thinking
- **Learn to earn** — build solutions for others in exchange for money

Once you have agency, AI can't replace you — it becomes a tool to accelerate you.
