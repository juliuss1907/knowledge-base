# Dopamine Reward Loop

## Definition

Vòng lặp phần thưởng dopamine trong trading — dopamine tạo cảm giác phấn khích (high) từ small wins, dẫn đến addictive behavior khi trader chase cảm giác này.

## Key ideas

- **Mechanism:** Dopamine gives the high từ trading wins
- **The loop:** Small wins → miss runner → FOMO → impulsive trades → lose more → lower standards → trading becomes addiction
- **Not about money:** "You've never actually been chasing money. You've been chasing relief."
- **Addiction signs:**
  - Trading to feel something (not to win)
  - PnL determines peace
  - Losing 30% không còn shock
- **The fix:** Sometimes the strongest thing là absolutely nothing — just stop
