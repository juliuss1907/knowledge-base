# Hippocampal Replay

## Definition

Biological process trong giấc ngủ — chuyển short-term hippocampal memories thành long-term cortical synaptic weights — inspiration cho LLM Sleep mechanism.

## Key ideas

- Occurs during sleep, không interfere wake-time responsiveness
- Replay và consolidation: strengthen memory traces
- Offline process allows deep computation
- Foundation của LLM Sleep concept
