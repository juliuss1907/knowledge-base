# Four Stages of the Market Cycle

## Definition

Bốn giai đoạn của chu kỳ thị trường theo phương pháp Stan Weinstein, giúp xác định khi nào nên aggressive, khi nào cautious, và khi nào protect capital.

## Key ideas

- **Stage 1 (Accumulation):** Accumulation bắt đầu, leadership có thể start improving, strategy là watch/prepare/build watchlist. Informed investors buy quietly while the crowd is bearish. Price moves sideways with low volatility.
- **Stage 2 (Markup/Uptrend):** Price confirm strength bằng cách vượt above key moving averages, form higher highs and higher lows, attract volume on rallies - đây là lúc enter/add/hold winning positions. Trend-followers enter, optimism grows.
- **Stage 3 (Distribution/Topping):** Momentum weaken, distribution xuất hiện, market bắt đầu show signs of topping - trim weaker positions, reduce exposure, become more selective. Smart money starts selling to the euphoria crowd.
- **Stage 4 (Markdown/Downtrend):** Trend broken, selling pressure heavy, capital preservation là priority - focus on cash, defense, or shorting when appropriate. Price falls, pessimism turns to panic.
- Mỗi stage có characteristic volume patterns, price action, và sentiment signatures riêng. Nhận diện đúng stage giúp biết khi nào aggressive, khi nào cautious.
- Dalio's "How the Economic Machine Works" framework maps these phases to the broader debt cycle: deleveraging creates accumulation; growth feeds markup; tightening triggers distribution và mark-down.
- Most retail traders làm ngược: mua during distribution (khi news bullish, crowd euphoric) và bán during accumulation (khi news bearish, sentiment bleak).
- "Holy Grail" trade là identify transition from accumulation to mark-up - buy early in uptrend với widest potential reward và tightest stop.
- Combining this cycle với Dow Theory, volume analysis, và Big Five forces framework cho multi-factor entry system.
