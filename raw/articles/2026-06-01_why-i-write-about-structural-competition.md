# Why I Write About Structural Competition

**Author:** Leon Liao (20+ years equity research/investment, global institutions and China)
**Source:** Substack — Leon Liao
**Date:** 2026-02-26
**URL:** https://open.substack.com/pub/leonliao/p/why-i-write-about-structural-competition

---

## Summary

Modern history is often told through familiar binary pairs: democracy vs. dictatorship, markets vs. state, freedom vs. control. These distinctions matter, but they don't explain enough. The real question is whether a society can organize capital, labor, technology, infrastructure, institutions, and external conditions into a self-reinforcing system to create capability. The core of 21st century global competition is no longer about which institutional label wins. It is competition between different systems' ability to organize actual capability.

---

## Core Argument

### The 4-Layer Framework

Leon Liao tracks 4 layers to assess system strength:

**1. Technology — shifts production frontier**
- AI changes the economics of cognition
- Semiconductors define computational power
- Energy sets the cost ceiling
- Not just advancement, but *integration at scale* to lower unit costs.

**2. Institutions — determines whether scale is feasible**
- Capital allocation, regulatory design, industrial policy, risk tolerance, corporate incentives
- "Technology sets potential; institutions determine realization."

**3. Industrial scale — restructures power before it becomes visible**
- Supply chain control, standards, cost curves, resilience
- When scale crosses thresholds, power shifts silently

**4. Capital markets — shows whether the system is priced, mispriced, or misunderstood**
- Market is part of the system, not an objective external measure

The entire Substack is organized into 13 fixed series, each tracking a piece of the system:

1. **China AI System** — AI as an industrial system (inference economics, cost compression, adoption flywheels)
2. **China Industrialization Secrets** — How China compresses industrialization time
3. **China Hard Tech Frontiers** — Space, robotics, nuclear, quantum, advanced manufacturing
4. **China's Institutional Capacity** — China as an operating system: central-local governance, state capacity
5. **Belt and Road & Global South** — Analysis by country, by deal
6. **Global Trade & Tech War** — Trade structure, supply chain geography, friction
7. **China Structural Challenges** — Demographics, property, debt, employment
8. **Rethinking the China Debate** — Auditing Washington narratives against data
9. **China Policy Watch** — Following Chinese policies in real-time
10. **US Structural Benchmarks** — Measuring America by capability structure benchmarks
11. **Global South System Building** — Developing countries trying to build systems
12. **Capital Market Signals** — Market readings through structural lens
13. **Deep Dives & Frameworks** — Foundational concepts

- Aimed at both Western and Chinese readers — not in any narrative camp
