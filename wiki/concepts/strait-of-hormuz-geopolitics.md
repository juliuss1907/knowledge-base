# Strait of Hormuz Geopolitics

## Definition

Strategic chokepoint cho global oil trade — 20% world's crude oil passes through — center của Iran War và UAE production disruption.

## Key ideas

- 20% global crude oil flow qua Hormuz
- Iran war: Tehran attack shipping → UAE production slump 44%
- Closure → physical impossibility move Gulf barrels
- When reopen: UAE ramp to 5M barrels/day unconstrained
