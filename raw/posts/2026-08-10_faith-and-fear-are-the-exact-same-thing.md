I find that my life improved a lot when I started diving deep into my fears, especially in a journal or on a whiteboard - almost like a chart.

What I've come to realize is...

faith and fear are the EXACT same thing - AND hold the same energetic potential.

Faith is having a blind, positive outlook on your future / outcomes, while simply FEAR is a negative outlook on your future / outcomes. THAT'S IT.

It's a powerful reframe, as we tend to forget 99.9% of our fears hold little to no merit.

Fear is simply rumination - it starts small, and we progressively find a very small amount of evidence to prove these larger, potential catastrophes right.

Fear CAN be killed. But it MUST be met head on.

IF you decide to shove away fear instead... It WILL ruminate in the background... and dictate EVERY last thought, belief, habit and action you ever take...

What you resist, persists.

How to neutralize the energy of fear...

Fear is always stored somewhere in your body - for me, mostly in my solar plexus and head.

Get out your journal:

- Identify your biggest fears - this can be few or many, there's no right or wrong answer - the more you identify, the better
- Create a chart - start with your most prominent fear - what if this materialized? Why do I fear this? What is the underlying cause? Am I afraid of letting others or myself down? What is the risk associated with this fear? What are the underlying fears?
- Energetic alchemy - once you've disgustingly explored your fears, it's time to alchemize that energy into faith. Get deep into meditation - see these fears materializing and feel where the energy is stored in your body - feel the energy with no bias, just feel it simply as it is - let the tension dissipate - you should feel lighter as a result of this exercise

Great questions to ask yourself during this meditation:

- Is this fear true?
- Is it ABSOLUTELY true?
- How would it feel to let this emotion go?

Simple said, simple done. Don't overcomplicate it.

You may not rid of ALL of your fear on your first try, but becoming aware of and neutralizing the energy behind your fear will be EXTREMELY fruitful.
