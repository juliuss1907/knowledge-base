# Financial Discipline

## Definition

Financial discipline là khả năng consistent manage money theo hệ thống có kỷ luật, thay vì spending based on impulses hoặc emotions. Khác với financial literacy (hiểu hệ thống), financial discipline là việc thực sự thực hành nó — budget, save, invest, và resist lifestyle inflation ngay cả khi income tăng.

## Key ideas

- **50-20-30-25-5 budget rule:** 50% needs (food, rent, electricity), 20% savings (emergency fund), 30% debt repayment, 25% investing (Roth IRA/stocks), 5% risky plays (start business)
- **Pay credit card full mỗi tháng:** Không trả full =浪费 money và hurt credit score — make it a habit
- **Skip flashy cars:** Buy used but reliable; consistently building wealth > short-term splurging — "true legends" build wealth over time
- **Invest retirement ngay từ đầu:** Bắt đầu sớm tạo compound advantage; 8%/year看起来 ít nhưng big picture hơn nhiều so với waiting
- **Do your own research:** Không bao giờ mua investment mà không tự research; someone pressuring you to invest = red flag
- **Nothing is free:** Không spending money thì spending time — young people thường không value time enough
- **Emergency fund first:** 20% savings vào high-interest savings account — your safety net trước khi invest
- **Debt repayment priority:** High-interest debt (credit cards, car finance) phải được pay off trước khi investing thêm
- **Build wealth over time:** Short-term success + splurging = broke; consistent building = financial freedom ở tuổi 30+
- **Any job > no job:** Being out of work takes mental toll; even washing cars for $10/hour > nothing — self-esteem + social connections
