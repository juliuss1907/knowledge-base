# The L1 Blockchain <> AI Lab Comparison

**Author:** Alok Vasudev (@AlokVasudev)

## Main Article

OpenAI is Bitcoin. Anthropic is Ethereum. All of the new labs are altcoins. Altlabs are raising gobs of money based on research whitepapers at high valuations justified on a relative basis to the category kings.

Bitcoin and OpenAI were immaculate conceptions. One of a kind founding stories with unique paths to success only possible for the first mover.

Ethereum and Anthropic have deep roots to the originals (Vitalik worked on Bitcoin; Dario worked for OpenAI). Both launched early and with enough differentiation to become credible alternatives before their spaces became overly crowded.

Bitcoin/OpenAI and Ethereum/Anthropic accrued outsized value in stunning fashion kicking off a cascade of would-be competition for a newly legible and large prize.

An altcoin/altlab frenzy ensued under the premise of a new "playbook": Raise a bunch of money, often across multiple financings, to fund an R&D program that will take years to yield a product that, when launched, is sufficiently better/different to turn a 2 horse race into an N horse race. Investors, viewing the category as structurally de-risked by existence proof of the originals, pile in cash relying on heuristics including perceived research novelty, localization ("Ethereum for China" was a thing), aggregated talent density, vertical focus, and early involvement of taste-making cabals (who btw have negotiated sweetheart deals for themselves).

There is a rapid, capex intensive scale-up/out of underpinning computational/energy infrastructure to support the new paradigm (miners :: neoclouds). Access to chips + energy, special relationships with companies/labs, and newly emerging proprietary operating capabilities define success for the winners. Here the theoretical similarities turned into practical realities as miners literally became neoclouds (Coreweave, Crusoe).

Fierce debates on long-term value accrual dynamics take place. Is it possible to compete against BTC/OAI/ETH/Ant (maximalism :: bitter lesson)? Are apps picking up pennies in front of the base layer steamroller (fat protocols :: thin wrappers)?

Rapid product and ecosystem development around the originals raises the table stakes for new entrants to succeed on launch. Bitcoin/Ethereum have clients, SDKs, dev tooling, middleware, etc. OpenAI/Anthropic have harnesses, integrations, partnerships, subsidies, etc.

So what happened in the aftermath of the L1 blockchain frenzy? Many never launched, the majority of those that did never accomplished anything of significance, a handful broke out to establish a "tier 2", and a select few have a shot on goal for a big durable prize (e.g. Solana). The "correct" investment strategy, in hindsight, was to fund everything you thought would launch.

One important distinction for blockchains is that investment liquidity occurred at or shortly after product launch (part of the deal if your product is a coin). AI Labs, on the other hand, need to establish a business model and generate liquidity the old fashioned way. Perhaps the AI financial bubble is strong enough that the liquidity constraint relaxes somewhat (it certainly has so far via M&A, secondaries, and tenders), but liquidity will be an intensifying challenge for altlabs.

The comparison is undeniable. We'll see if the lessons (and the investment returns) are too.
