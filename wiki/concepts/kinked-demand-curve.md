# Kinked Demand Curve

## Definition

Economic model của oligopoly pricing — demand elastic trên price P (rivals don't follow), inelastic dưới P (rivals match cuts) — tạo ra kink tại equilibrium.

## Key ideas

- DP↑: Elastic demand — raise price → lose market share
- DP↓: Inelastic demand — cut price → rivals match, gain little
- Kink at P,Q creates stable equilibrium
- OPEC operates exactly như model này
