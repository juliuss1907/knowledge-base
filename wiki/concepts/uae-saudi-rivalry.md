# UAE-Saudi Rivalry

## Definition

Simmering competition giữa Abu Dhabi và Riyadh — từ allies thành rivals trên oil policy, regional geopolitics, và Vision 2030 competition.

## Key ideas

- Disagreements: OPEC production baselines, quota calculations
- Yemen war: backing opposing sides → Saudi bombing UAE-backed shipment
- Economic rivalry: race diversify, attract capital, become financial hub
- Trust gap sau December incident "might never be repaired"
