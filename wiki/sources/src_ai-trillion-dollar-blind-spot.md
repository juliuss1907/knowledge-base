## Summary

Companies are racing to embed AI into their products while ignoring the most critical customer touchpoint: the static landing page. The traditional website cannot have conversations or adapt to context, creating high friction for potential customers trying to determine product relevance.

## Key points

1. **The blind spot:** Companies rebuild everything with AI except the front door (landing pages)
2. **Static website limitations:**
   - Single page must speak to everyone (founders, buyers, engineers, executives)
   - Cannot adapt to context
   - Cannot answer follow-up questions
   - Cannot tell what matters to each visitor

3. **Current "solutions" are insufficient:**
   - Rewriting copy
   - A/B testing button colors
   - Adding chatbots (high friction)
   - "Book a call" button (low conversion)

4. **The real issue:** Interface problem — static pages cannot have conversations

5. **AI answer engines are absorbing:** The job websites were built for (helping people understand products)

6. **Opportunity:** Rebuilding discovery layer with AI-powered, conversational interfaces
