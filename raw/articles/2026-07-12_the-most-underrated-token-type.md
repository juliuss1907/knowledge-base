# The most underrated token type

**Author:** a16z crypto
**Source:** Substack
**Published:** 2026-07-11

---

## Core Thesis

In a16z crypto's token taxonomy (7 types), the least explored and most underappreciated is the **arcade token**: a token with a relatively stable value within a specific software or product ecosystem, often managed by an issuer (like a company).

Arcade tokens are the blockchain-based equivalent of airline miles, credit card points, and in-game digital gold — internal currencies that support the functioning of a marketplace economy. But unlike traditional centralized versions, onchain arcade tokens are open, interoperable, and composable.

---

## What Are Arcade Tokens?

The metaphor: you go to an arcade, trade cash for physical tokens, then use those tokens to play games. The tokens let you participate in the arcade's economy. Arcade tokens have a **relatively stable value** within their economic system — whether within a single service or across multiple services.

Key distinction: **Arcade tokens are for spending, not investment or speculation.** Unlike network or security tokens acquired for investment returns, arcade tokens provide utility. They don't offer, promise, or imply financial returns — they're typically outside the scope of U.S. securities laws.

Alternative names: "utility tokens" (avoided because other token types also have utility), "points" (implies private ledgers), "loyalty tokens" (describes just one application).

---

## Economic Mechanics

Arcade tokens enable builders to **print and provision value** within a digital economy — incentivizing user behavior, bootstrapping early growth, and generating network effects without relying on external capital or speculative demand.

### Supply Control
If twice as many customers show up, mint roughly twice as many tokens. Why turn people away when you can meet demand?

### Dynamic Pricing
Adjust token prices, adjust token-denominated purchase prices, or both. Discount during low-demand, reward during peak moments.

### Price Ceiling via Faucet
Tokens freely available at a pre-specified price via a "faucet" or "spigot" — like a token vending machine. This sets a **price ceiling** the market value should never exceed.

### Price Floor via Buyback
The issuer guarantees buyback at slightly below faucet price — establishing a floor under which the price should not drop.

### Network Effects + Loyalty
Users holding tokens are more attached to the brand. When they go home with leftover tokens, they carry an incentive to come back. Can give bonuses and perks for desired actions.

### Economic Control
Burn tokens upon redemption, track liabilities onchain, implement monetary policy akin to central banking — all while keeping supply and pricing within a predictable band.

---

## Comparison with Stablecoins

Both facilitate economic transactions with relatively stable value, but arcade tokens offer greater flexibility:
- Print tokens on demand
- Provide grants and subsidies to users, developers, and network participants
- Encourage participants to remain within the economy (like airline miles vs cash rebates)
- More options for monetization: direct sales, subscription bundles, promotional campaigns
- Programmatic controls: restricted transferability, depreciation/expiration, tying redemptions to specific goods

---

## The Onchain Advantage: Interoperability

Unlike traditional loyalty systems that trap value within closed ecosystems, blockchain-based arcade tokens can be permissionlessly shared, accepted, and redeemed by multiple players — even among competitors. Users can carry loyalty across services, and status can be transferred easily.

This enables **coopetition** instead of classic competition: a local coffee shop and Starbucks could both gain from accepting the same token. Shared loyalty programs mediated by arcade tokens can grow the overall pie rather than fighting over slices.

---

## Real-World Example: Blackbird's $FLY

$FLY creates a loyalty program for restaurants, similar to Starbucks Stars but usable across many different restaurants. Patrons receive tokens when purchasing at any Blackbird network restaurant and can redeem them for discounts at any participating restaurant — all happening onchain without restaurants interacting directly.

## When to Use Arcade Tokens

**Good fit for:** Spend-centric economies or physical-world integrations

Benefits include:
- **Price stability:** Ceiling and floor mechanisms + controlled issuance
- **Usability:** Intuitive, stable value
- **Accounting clarity:** Cost on balance sheet = opportunity cost of what they're redeemable for
- **Control:** Central-bank-like management

**Not suitable for:** Situations requiring speculative assets (e.g., L1 networks with network tokens)

### Arcade Tokens as Precursors to Network Tokens
A marketplace might bootstrap participation with an arcade token and later introduce a network token as it decentralizes — arcade tokens serve as an **on-ramp** that catalyzes early demand.

## Regulatory Precedent: Quarters

Pocketful of Quarters (blockchain gaming platform) received a no-action letter from the SEC in July 2019 — the agency recognized Quarters were used solely for gaming participation, not speculation or investment.
