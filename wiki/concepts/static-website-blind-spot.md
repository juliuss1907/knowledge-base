# Static Website Blind Spot

## Definition

Static Website Blind Spot là xu hướng các công ty tập trung tích hợp AI vào sản phẩm nội bộ trong khi bỏ qua trang landing page tĩnh — touchpoint quan trọng nhất trong customer journey — tạo ra friction cao cho potential customers.

## Key Ideas

- **The paradox:** Companies embed AI everywhere except where customers first see them
- **Static page problems:**
  - One-size-fits-all content
  - No contextual adaptation
  - No conversational capability
  - High friction to determine product relevance

- **Current band-aids:**
  - Copy rewriting
  - Headline swapping
  - A/B testing
  - Chatbot corners (high friction)
  - "Book a call" (low conversion)

- **The shift:** AI answer engines are absorbing the discovery job websites were built for

## Opportunity

Rebuild the discovery layer with:
- AI-powered conversational interfaces
- Context-aware content
- Dynamic personalization
- Lower friction pathways
