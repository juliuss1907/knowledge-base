# Goal-Directed Learning

## Definition

Goal-directed learning là approach learning xuất phát từ clear goals. Goals tạo error signal (gap giữa current và desired state), error signal tạo filter (what's relevant), filter tạo relevance, relevance tạo retention. Without goals, learning lacks direction và bị tràn ngập noise.

## Key ideas

- Must be deep intrinsically generated goal, không phải superficial goal (như New Year's resolution)
- Goal tạo frame để evaluate what matters
- Without goal: brain filters out opportunities không liên quan đến conditioned path
- Person conditioned for "school → job" path won't notice money-making opportunities outside jobs
- Brain chỉ "see" what relevant đến current goals
- To become obsessed: visualize outcome của current actions if don't change
- Let potent emotion fill you và redirect toward better direction
- Extreme changes > many small changes cho neuroplasticity
- "Burn the boats" — commit fully để force adaptation
