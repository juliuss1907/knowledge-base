# Rot Economy

## Definition

Hiện tượng công ty tech tự nguyện làm suy giảm core products để làm hài lòng shareholders — chuyển từ "make good products at fair price" thành "display most stock price growth" — biến services useful/beloved thành hollow shells.

## Key ideas

- **Origin**: decades of neoliberal thinking — company drained of meaning beyond "domination and extraction"
- **Symptom**: growth at all costs, short-term gains, quarterly stock price over happy customers
- **Tech examples**: Facebook deliberately broken, Google Search intentionally made worse, Meta scam ads
- **Incentive structure**: executives rewarded for growth → culture of dominance and extraction
- **Management shift**: không còn "do work" mà là "establish cultures of dominance"
- **CEO measurement**: không phải happy customers hay revenue today, mà "revenue might be tomorrow"
- **Media capture**: Business Idiots kiểm soát cả media và markets, twist "good company" definition
- Contrast với sustainable business: loyal market, good products, fair price
